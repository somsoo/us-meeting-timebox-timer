Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Do auditory alerts work in background browser tabs?

Yes. Using the Web Audio API, chime notifications will ring clearly at the 30-second warning and end-of-time mark even when this tab is hidden behind Zoom or Google Meet windows.

How does timeboxing optimize screen sharing during scrum?

The high-contrast dark HUD display makes remaining time instantly legible across remote screens without taking up full display estate, ensuring disciplined adherence to agile cadence.

Is any corporate meeting information transmitted to cloud servers?

No. This tool operates on a 100% serverless local execution model. All agendas, speaker names, and durations remain confidential within your local browser memory.
